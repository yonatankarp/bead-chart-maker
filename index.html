Bead Chart Maker

Load a picture and get a pegboard chart in your own bead colours, split across as many boards as it needs. Pick a colour to see only where it goes, or select any part of it to see its size in rows and columns. Everything runs in your browser; your pictures never leave your computer.

My beads

With "My beads" chosen, the chart uses only the ticked colours. Edit a code, a name or a colour, or select a row and click a pile in the photo to set its colour from the real beads.

Add from the range

Click a colour to add it to my beads, or click again to take it out. Colours are measured by the community, so they're close, not exact.

Match from a photo

Photograph a pile from each bag on white paper in daylight. Click the paper once to correct the light, then select a row on the left and click its pile. Without a selected row, a click adds a new colour.
